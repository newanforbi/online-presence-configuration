# People-search and data-broker opt-outs

These sites copy court and SOS records, then display **aka** lines that put Newanforbi on a Brendan Ngwa Nforbi profile (and the reverse). They are often what Google actually shows. Opting out here does more for the SERP than arguing with UniCourt.

Search each site for:

- Brendan Ngwa Nforbi, Stockton, CA
- The other spelling, Stockton, CA
- Altamont Freight LLC

If a listing is you, copy its URL, then use that site’s official removal page. After the live page is gone, file an [outdated content](https://search.google.com/search-console/remove-outdated-content) request so Google drops the snippet.

Re-check TruePeopleSearch, FastPeopleSearch, and Spokeo every 90 days. They relist from the same public records.

## California (you live here)

California residents can demand deletion from registered data brokers:

- [California Privacy Protection Agency — data brokers](https://cppa.ca.gov/)
- [Privacy.ca.gov](https://privacy.ca.gov/)

When a broker’s form asks for the legal name, you may need to give the spelling they stored so they can find the record. That is a private form, not a public webpage. Do not publish that pairing on any page you own.

## Business-data reprints

[BizProfile’s Altamont Freight LLC page](https://www.bizprofile.net/ca/modesto/altamont-freight-llc) lists the professional name **and** a residential address. That address is a cross-name glue.

- Ask Google to hide the **address** via Results about you (commercial site, not `.gov`).
- Look on the page for a privacy / removal / “this is my business” contact and request they stop showing the residential street. They may refuse because it came from a SOS filing.
- The California Secretary of State record itself will remain.

## What not to pay for

Paid “reputation management” SEO that promises to delete court records is selling something Google will not do. Paid data-broker services (Incogni, Optery, OneRep) only automate the table above. They are optional; the official URLs are free.
